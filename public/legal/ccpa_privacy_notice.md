**Effective Date:** July 29, 2026

## Section 1: Introduction and Purpose

Welcome to Toyland. This document constitutes a formal agreement and legal guideline regarding "California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) Privacy Policy". The main objective of this policy is to outline our standards, obligations, rights, and compliance procedures to ensure a safe, clean, and transparent shopping environment for families, children, and retail consumers.
