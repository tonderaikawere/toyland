# Supply Chain Transparency & Ethical Sourcing Policy

**Effective Date:** July 29, 2026
**Document Version:** 4.10.12
**Reference Code:** LAW-TOY-1024

## Section 1: Introduction and Purpose

Welcome to Toyland. This document constitutes a formal agreement and legal guideline regarding "Supply Chain Transparency & Ethical Sourcing Policy". The main objective of this policy is to outline our standards, obligations, rights, and compliance procedures to ensure a safe, clean, and transparent shopping environment for families, children, and retail consumers.
